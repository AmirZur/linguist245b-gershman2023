# Descriptions of columns in data file

Each row is data from one participant. 

Within each condition, the order of presentation of trials was randomized. 

Within each trial, participants saw either version A of that trial, or version B of that trial. 

Within each trial, the answer order was randomized. 

* `risk_increase_A`: Participant responses to the statement `Themaglin is associated with a higher risk of Rebosen`
* `risk_increase_B`: Participant responses to the statement `Rebosen is associated with a higher risk of Themaglin`
* `prob_increase_A`: Participant responses to the statement `Pneuben is associated with higher probability of Efogen`
* `prob_increase_B`: Participant responses to the statement `Efogen is associated with higher probability of Pneuben`
* `risk_decrease_A`: Participant responses to the statement `Agoriv is associated with a lower risk of Ceflar`
* `risk_decrease_B`: Participant responses to the statement `Ceflar is associated with a lower risk of Agoriv`
* `prob_decrease_A`: Participant responses to the statement `Turilin is associated with lower probability of Laurem`
* `prob_decrease_B`: Participant responses to the statement `Laurem is associated with lower probability of Turilin`
* `assoc_A`: Participant responses to the statement `Denoden is associated with Flembers`
* `assoc_B`: Participant responses to the statement `Flembers is associated with Denoden`
* `screen_explain`: Participant responses to a question asking them to explain the purpose of the study, in their own words. Used for screening low-effort responses.
* `comments`: Participant feedback at the end of the study, any and all comments were welcome. 


Within each condition, the order of presentation of trials was randomized (e.g., some participants saw `Themaglin is associated with a higher risk of Rebosen` first, others saw `Pneuben is associated with higher probability of Efogen` first).

Within each trial, the answer order was randomized.
